# Payments and Gift Cards

## Accepted payment methods
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, EFTPOS, cash, Apple Pay and Google Pay. We do **not** accept cheques or cryptocurrency.

## Split payments
You can pay with up to **two payment methods** per transaction.

## Surcharges
There is **no surcharge** on EFTPOS or cash. A **1.5% surcharge** applies to American Express payments.

## Gift cards
- Gift cards are valid for **3 years** from the date of purchase.
- Gift cards can be used in store only.
- Gift cards **cannot be refunded for cash** and cannot be exchanged for another gift card.
- A lost or stolen gift card can only be replaced if you have the original receipt and the card number.
- Gift card purchases do not earn loyalty points.

## Price match
We match a lower advertised price from a local Australian retailer on the **same item in stock**, for up to **7 days after your purchase** with the receipt. Clearance items and online-only marketplace sellers are excluded.
