# Stock and Reordering Procedures (INTERNAL, STAFF ONLY)

## Reordering
When an item's stock falls **below its reorder level**, a purchase order should be raised. Check the low stock list at the start of each shift.

## Purchase order approval
- Orders **under $500**: shift supervisor can approve.
- Orders **$500 and over**: a manager must approve.
- Orders to a **new supplier** always need manager approval, whatever the amount.

## Receiving stock
Check delivered quantities against the purchase order **within 24 hours** of arrival. Record the received quantity in the system. Report any shortage, damage or wrong item to the supplier within **48 hours**.

## Stock adjustments
Every manual adjustment needs a **reason** recorded (for example: damaged, stocktake correction, theft). Adjustments over **10 units** need manager approval. The audit history is kept and cannot be edited.

## Stocktake
A full stocktake is done on the **first Sunday of each quarter**. Count sheets are signed by two staff members.

## Shrinkage
Report suspected theft to the store manager the same day. Do not confront customers.
