# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Standard returns
You can return an item within **30 days** of purchase for a full refund to your original payment method if it is **unopened, unused and you have your receipt**.

## Opened or used items
If an item is opened or used but not faulty, we offer an **exchange or store credit** (not a cash refund) within **14 days** of purchase, with the receipt.

## Faulty items
If an item is faulty, you are entitled to a repair, replacement or refund. There is no fixed time limit, but the fault must appear within a reasonable time for that kind of product. Bring the item and your receipt. Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

## Clearance and sale items
Items marked **Clearance** are **exchange only** unless faulty. Items marked **Sale** follow the standard return rules.

## Returns without a receipt
We can offer **store credit only**, valued at the **lowest price the item sold for in the last 90 days**. Photo ID is required and the return is recorded against the ID.

## Large refunds
Any refund over **$200** needs approval from a manager before it is processed.

## Gift cards
Gift cards are never refunded for cash. See the Payments and Gift Cards document.
