# Product Care and Warranty

## Warranty periods
- Electronics and accessories with batteries: **12 months** from purchase.
- Furniture and homewares: **24 months** from purchase.
- Clothing and soft goods: **6 months** for manufacturing defects.

## Making a warranty claim
Bring the item and your **receipt** to the store. We assess the item in store, and where needed send it to the supplier. Typical turnaround for supplier repairs is **10 to 15 business days**. We will offer a loan item where one is available.

## What warranty does not cover
Accidental damage, water damage, wear and tear from normal use, damage from unauthorised repairs, and items used for commercial purposes.

## Care tips
- **Electronics**: keep away from moisture. Use only the supplied or approved charger. Do not leave batteries fully drained for long periods.
- **Furniture**: wipe with a slightly damp cloth, avoid direct sunlight for long periods, and use coasters on timber surfaces.
- **Concrete and stone products**: seal annually and avoid harsh acids or abrasive cleaners.
- **Metal products**: dry after washing to avoid rust and polish with a soft cloth.
