# Loyalty Program

## Earning points
Members earn **1 point for every $1 spent** on eligible purchases. Gift card purchases and gift card payments do not earn points.

## Redeeming points
Every **100 points** can be redeemed for **$5 off** a purchase. Points cannot be redeemed for cash.

## Tiers
- **Silver**: 500 points earned in a calendar year. Members get early access to sales.
- **Gold**: 2,000 points earned in a calendar year. Members get early access to sales and **free alterations on in-store purchases**, plus double points on their birthday week.

Tiers reset each 1 January, based on points earned the previous year.

## Birthday bonus
Members receive **50 bonus points** in their birthday month. Gold members also get double points during their birthday week.

## Expiry
Points **expire after 12 months of no activity** on the account. Any purchase or redemption counts as activity.

## Returns and points
If you return an item, the points earned on it are removed from your balance.
